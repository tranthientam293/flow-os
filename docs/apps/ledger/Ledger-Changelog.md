# Ledger: Changelog

Every Ledger release, oldest first. Copied from the app's [`README.md`](https://github.com/tranthientam293/flow-os/blob/master/src/apps/ledger/README.md#changelog), which is the source of truth; when they differ, the README wins.

- **0.1.0** (Oct 14, 2026): first version. The app shows its name, with a Back to flowOS button in the header.
- **0.2.0** (Oct 14, 2026): the header shows the app name and a user icon, like Roster. The user icon opens an account menu with your email and Back to flowOS, which replaces the home button.
- **0.2.1** (Oct 14, 2026): Back to flowOS uses the platform's `useBackToPlatform` hook from `@/hooks`; the app's own copy is removed.
- **0.3.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Settings** page (`/apps/ledger/settings`), opened from **Settings** in the account menu: your account (flowOS sign-in email) and Back to flowOS. The app name in the header links to Home; unknown paths go back to Home.
- **0.4.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Add expense** button on Home opens the expense modal: amount in đồng (whole đồng, grouped with dots, ₫ suffix), category, note (up to 200 characters) and date (today by default). Categories are modeled on Money Lover's expense categories (`constants/categories.ts`): Food & Beverage, Transportation, Bills & Utilities, Shopping, Family, Health & Fitness, Entertainment, Education, Insurances, Gifts & Donations, Investment, Pay Interest, Outgoing Transfer and Other Expense, several with subcategories (e.g. Bills & Utilities → Electricity Bill). The picker is searchable and shows each category's icon, with subcategories indented under their parent. Expenses aren't saved yet: Save only closes the modal.
- **0.5.0** (Oct 14, 2026): expenses are saved to the new `ledger_expenses` table. Home lists the month's expenses grouped by day (newest first, each day with its total), with a month picker (future months disabled) and the month's total. **Add expense** sits next to the total on desktop and is a full-width button fixed to the bottom of the screen on phones. After saving, Home shows the month the expense was added to.
- **0.6.0** (Oct 14, 2026): edit and delete expenses. Click an expense in the list to open it in the expense modal (**Edit expense**), change it and Save; **Delete** in the modal removes it after a confirmation. After saving, Home shows the month the expense is in.
- **0.7.0** (Oct 14, 2026): each expense in the list has **Edit** (pencil) and **Delete** (trash, danger color) icon buttons. Delete asks for confirmation, then removes the expense without opening the modal. Clicking the rest of the row still opens the editor.
- **0.7.1** (Oct 14, 2026): on Home, the month picker moves below the month's total and **Add expense** (which stays next to the total on desktop).
- **0.7.2** (Oct 14, 2026): pickers work on phones. The category picker has no search box on phones (so the keyboard doesn't open and cover the list) and a shorter list; the date and month pickers don't open the keyboard (pick from the calendar); the note uses 16px text on phones so iOS doesn't zoom in.
- **0.8.0** (Oct 14, 2026): the amount field takes a sum, e.g. `50.000 + 20.000 − 5.000` (+, −, ×, ÷; × and ÷ first), and shows the result below it as you type; the saved amount is the result, rounded to whole đồng. Phones open the number keyboard, and a row of **+ − × ÷ =** buttons under the field adds operators without closing it; on a computer + - * / can be typed too. **=** or leaving the field replaces the sum with its result. A result of 0 or less shows “The amount must be more than 0”.
- **0.8.1** (Oct 14, 2026): Enter no longer saves the expense modal; only **Save** does. In the amount field Enter shows the result (like **=**), and in the note it adds a new line.
- **0.8.2** (Oct 14, 2026): Home and the expense list memoize the month total and the day grouping (with each day's total), so they're only recalculated when the expenses change. No visible change.
- **0.9.0** (Oct 14, 2026): the category opens in its own **Select category** modal instead of a dropdown, like Money Lover's category screen. The field shows the chosen category's icon and name; the modal lists every category with its icon, subcategories indented under their parent, with a search box (matching a subcategory keeps its parent visible) and a check on the chosen one. Picking a category closes the modal. Replaces `CategorySelect` with `CategoryPicker`.
- **0.9.1** (Oct 14, 2026): deleting an expense (trash button in the list, or **Delete** in the edit modal) asks in a confirmation dialog (“Delete this expense?”, like Back to flowOS) instead of a small popover. Its Delete button spins until the expense is deleted; the dialog stays open if it fails. Shared through `hooks/useConfirmDelete.ts`.
- **0.10.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Dashboard**, modeled on Money Lover's home, is the new home page (`/apps/ledger`); the expense list moves to **Expenses** (`/apps/ledger/expenses`). The header gets **Dashboard** / **Expenses** tabs. The dashboard shows the month's total compared with last month (same days so far for the current month), the daily average and number of expenses; a daily spending column chart with tooltips; top spending per category (top five, then “Everything else”) with amounts, shares and bars; and the 5 most recent expenses with **See all**. The chosen month lives in the URL (`?month=YYYY-MM`), shared by both pages. One expense modal (add, edit, delete) now serves every page through `ExpenseEditorProvider`; its title no longer flips to “Add expense” while an edit closes.
- **0.11.0** (Oct 14, 2026): the daily spending chart uses **Recharts** (`recharts` 3.10, new dependency) instead of hand-built columns: same look (brand green bars with a 4px rounded top, hairline horizontal grid, round axis maximum with compact vi-VN labels, days 1, 5, 10, … on the x axis) and a tooltip with the day and amount on hover, keyboard focus or tap. Recharts is only in Ledger's lazy-loaded bundle.
- **0.12.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Top spending** on the dashboard gets a doughnut chart (Recharts) of each category's share of the month, with the total in the middle and a tooltip (name, amount, share). The list beside it doubles as the legend: each row has its slice's color dot and its bar in that color. Five slice colors from the dataviz reference palette, validated against Ledger's card in light and dark (`constants/chart.ts`); “Everything else” is gray. Side by side on desktop, stacked on phones.
- **0.13.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Compared with last month** chart on the dashboard, under the summary: spending so far, day by day, for the chosen month (brand green; it stops at today in the current month) against the month before (gray), with a legend naming both months. Hover or tap a day to see both totals by that day and how much more or less was spent (more in the danger color, less in green).
- **0.13.1** (Oct 14, 2026): **Top spending** rows drop their bars; each row shows the color dot, icon, name, number of expenses, amount and share.
- **0.13.2** (Oct 14, 2026): **Top spending** rows no longer show the number of expenses (× n).
- **0.14.0** (Oct 14, 2026): every dashboard section always shows, with its own empty message when it has nothing to show, instead of one message replacing the whole dashboard: **Compared with last month** when neither month has spending (last month's line still shows when only this month is empty), **Daily spending** and **Top spending** when the month has no spending, **Recent expenses** when it has no expenses (See all stays). The summary shows 0 ₫. Empty messages keep each chart's height.
- **0.15.0** (Oct 14, 2026): the month picker (Dashboard and Expenses) gets **‹** previous month and **›** next month buttons around it and a **Today** button for the current month; next and Today are disabled on the current month (future months aren't allowed).
- **0.16.0** (Oct 14, 2026): Ledger's dialogs (expense, category picker, delete confirmation) fade in and scale up from the center on desktop instead of zooming from the click point (`ledger.css`, `transitionName` from `constants/modal.ts`); on phones they still slide up as a bottom sheet, and with reduced motion they simply fade.
- **0.16.1** (Oct 14, 2026): the **Select category** dialog is as wide as the expense dialog (480px on desktop, `MODAL_WIDTH` in `constants/modal.ts`).
- **0.16.2** (Oct 14, 2026): the **Select category** dialog is also as tall as the expense dialog it opens from (measured when it opens); the category list fills it and scrolls.
- **0.17.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Monthly limit** in Settings, saved to the new `ledger_settings` table (follows your account). The Dashboard summary shows how much of it you've used, what's left (or how much over, in the danger color) with a bar, and for the current month about how much a day is left; without a limit it links to Settings.
- **0.18.0** (Oct 14, 2026): every category has its own color. Its icon badge (category picker, expense lists, Recent expenses, Top spending) uses a Tailwind color family set per category in `constants/categories.ts` (`tone`, light and dark), instead of brand green for all. A parent and its subcategories each get a different color; Other Expense and “Everything else” are gray.
- **0.18.1** (Oct 14, 2026): in **Top spending** the category icons are gray, so the only color in each row is its legend dot (the doughnut slice color) and an icon color never clashes with the slice.
- **0.19.0** (Oct 14, 2026): the **Top spending** doughnut colors each slice with its category's color (Tailwind's 500 step of its `tone`, `CATEGORY_TONE_COLOR`), so the slice, the legend dot and the icon match; icons there are in color again. “Everything else” stays gray. These colors aren't colorblind-checked as a set (close pairs such as Family / Investment), so the list's names, amounts and shares still carry identity. Also fixes a missing slice color when there were exactly six categories.
- **0.19.1** (Oct 14, 2026): the **Top spending** list drops the color dot after each category icon; the icon, in the same color family as its slice, is the legend key.
- **0.20.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Compared with last month** is now a Money Lover-style column chart: last month's whole total next to this month's, each labelled with its amount, in place of the two running-total lines.
- **0.21.0** (Oct 14, 2026): on phones the top bar moves to the bottom and joins **Add expense**: a bottom bar with **Dashboard**, **Expenses**, a raised round **+** in the middle, **Settings** and **flowOS** (Back to flowOS). It replaces the full-width Add expense button and the top header on phones; desktop is unchanged.
- **0.21.1** (Oct 14, 2026): the bottom bar's **flowOS** button uses a log-out icon instead of the house.
- **0.21.2** (Oct 14, 2026): the ring around the bottom bar's round **+** is a soft brand green (`ring-brand-soft`) instead of the bar's own color.
- **0.21.3** (Oct 14, 2026): the bottom bar's top edge and border bulge up around the raised **+**, so the button sits inside the bar's outline instead of floating above it.
- **0.22.0** (Oct 14, 2026): clearer expense modal buttons. They sit below a divider; the main button says what it does (**Add expense** or **Save changes**) and, when editing, stays disabled until something changes; Delete is a quieter red text button on the left; while saving, Cancel and Delete are disabled. On phones Cancel and the main button share the row equally at a taller (44px) size.
- **0.23.0** (Oct 14, 2026): every page shows its title and a one-line description at the top (`PageTitle`): **Dashboard** (“Your month at a glance.”), **Expenses** (“Everything you spent this month, day by day.”) and **Settings**. Phones have no top bar, so this names the page there.
- **0.24.0** (Oct 14, 2026): on desktop, when the **Add expense** button scrolls out of view (Dashboard, Expenses), a round floating **+** fades in at the bottom right and fades out when the button is back. Phones keep the + in the bottom bar.
- **0.25.0** (Oct 14, 2026): when the page's **Add expense** button scrolls out of view on desktop, **Add expense** appears in the header (next to the account menu) instead of the floating round **+**, and goes away when the button is back. The page button hands its action to the header through the expense editor context (`headerAdd`).
- **0.26.0** (Oct 14, 2026): a footer at the bottom of every page: “copyright by tranthientam293” (on phones, above the bottom bar).
- **0.26.1** (Oct 14, 2026): the footer reads “© <year> tranthientam293” in smaller, lighter text, with more space around it so the bottom bar's raised + never covers it on phones.
- **0.26.2** (Oct 14, 2026): the footer reads “Original by tranthientam2903 · © <year> flowOS”.
- **0.27.0** (Oct 14, 2026): **Last 12 months** chart on the Dashboard (Recharts `BarChart`): spending per month for the 12 months ending with the chosen month, the chosen month in brand green and the rest in light gray, a dashed line at the monthly average (over months with spending, also written above the chart), a tooltip with the month and amount, and a click on a column opens that month. Data comes from a new `yearExpensesQueryOptions` (amount and day only, fetched in pages of 1,000 rows); it refreshes when expenses are saved or deleted.
- **0.27.1** (Oct 14, 2026): the **Monthly limit** field in Settings takes a plain amount only: no + − × ÷ = buttons, and typed operators are ignored (`AmountInput operators={false}`). The expense modal keeps sums.
- **0.27.2** (Oct 14, 2026): **Compared with last month** labels its columns by month number (“Month 9”, “Month 10”) instead of Last month / This month. **Last 12 months** labels months as MM/YYYY (“09/2026”) on the axis and in the tooltip; on narrow screens labels that would overlap are skipped, keeping the first and last.
- **0.27.3** (Oct 14, 2026): **Compared with last month** labels its columns MM/YYYY (“09/2026”, “10/2026”), like Last 12 months.
- **0.27.4** (Oct 14, 2026): **Last 12 months** labels months MM/YY (“09/26”) on the axis and in the tooltip, short enough for every label to fit more often on phones.
- **0.27.5** (Oct 14, 2026): **Last 12 months** is back to MM/YYYY (“09/2026”) on the axis and in the tooltip, matching Compared with last month.
- **0.27.6** (Oct 14, 2026): **Add expense** always opens empty (today's date, nothing else filled in) instead of keeping the last expense's amount, category and note. The expense modal starts fresh on every open.
- **0.27.7** (Oct 14, 2026): on phones the calendar of the **date** (expense modal) and **month** (Dashboard, Expenses) pickers opens like the dialogs: a bottom sheet at full width with rounded top corners, the page dimmed behind it, sliding up and down, with taller day cells. Tapping outside still closes it. Desktop keeps the dropdown (`PICKER_SHEET` in `constants/modal.ts`, styles in `ledger.css`).
- **0.27.8** (Oct 14, 2026): clicking an expense no longer opens the edit modal. On **Expenses** use the ✏️ (edit) and 🗑 (delete) buttons on each row; **Recent expenses** on the Dashboard is display only (**See all** opens Expenses).
